演講談到大腦的「預設模式網路」（default mode network）：當你沒有專注於任何事時，大腦其實很忙——不斷檢查環境是否安全、資源是否足夠，這是基本的生存模式。工作、薪水、人際、股市等擔憂，往往就在這種「放空」狀態下反覆出現。

當注意力被某件事抓住——例如極限運動或高度專注的活動——你會暫時離開預設模式網路，焦慮降到最低。冥想的本質是讓你專注於一件事；「心流」（flow）則是做自己最愛的事時高度專注、不困倦。許多睡眠不佳的人常在懊悔過去、擔憂未來；演講建議：肯定過去、期待未來——失敗也能成為經驗，練習感恩。

English key takeaways

  • The default mode network is what your brain does when you are not focused: it keeps checking whether your environment is safe and your resources are sufficient—basic survival mode.
  • Anxiety drops when something catches your attention and you become highly focused; you temporarily disconnect from the default mode network.
  • Meditation is about focusing on one thing; flow is being highly focused on what you love most—not sleepy, and sometimes losing track of time.
  • Find what you have wanted to do most since childhood; when you do that work, anxiety tends to be at its lowest—even if the task is not easy.
  • People who sleep poorly often regret the past and worry about the future; the speaker’s advice is to affirm the past and look forward to the future—gratitude and learning from failure.
