這場演講說明：夜間睡眠時心率與血氧劇烈波動後，血壓常在清晨達到高峰。晨間血壓特別高，可能是嚴重睡眠呼吸中止與交感神經過度活化的「紅旗」（red flag），尤其常見於 40–50 歲、需服用多種降壓藥卻控制不佳的男性。

阻塞缺氧時，肺血管收縮、右心負荷增加，心房被撐張後釋放心房利鈉肽（ANP），如同夜間被給予利尿劑，導致夜尿。夜尿不一定是攝水量或攝護腺肥大造成；攝護腺肥大主要影響排尿速度，難以解釋夜尿量。夜間體重明顯下降（如 1.5–2 公斤）常反映夜尿與呼吸中止嚴重度。另有患者出現休息或睡眠時心絞痛，心導管卻無阻塞，可能是血管痙攣。

English key takeaways

  • Morning hypertension can be a red flag for severe sleep apnea driven by overnight sympathetic overactivity—not just “daytime hypertension.”
  • Men in their 40s–50s may deny insomnia yet still be sleep-deprived; stubborn hypertension on multiple drugs often masks apnea.
  • Nocturia may reflect atrial natriuretic peptide (ANP) release after right-heart pressure rises during obstructive breaths—not simply evening water intake.
  • An enlarged prostate explains slow or difficult urination, but not necessarily a large overnight urine volume.
  • Large overnight weight loss (about 1.5–2 kg after emptying the bladder) and rest/sleep angina with clean vessels can signal severe apnea and coronary spasm.
